A SMALL BUT IMPORTANT GUARDRAIL

Local actions, protected.

Autowell adds a random CSRF token to each form and validates it against a SameSite=Strict cookie. This helps prevent another website from silently triggering settings changes, job imports, or application submissions through your open browser session.

It is one layer of protection—not a substitute for keeping your machine and browser secure, or reviewing which jobs you select.
